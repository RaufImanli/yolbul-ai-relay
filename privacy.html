Privacy Policy

Application Name: YolBul
Package Name: app.yolbul.mobile
Effective Date: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how YolBul ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, processes, and protects your information when you use our mobile application ("Application" or "Service"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding how your data is handled.

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

A. Location Data (Precise and Coarse)

To provide core turn-by-turn navigation, transit stop guidance, and nearby place discovery, the Application accesses your device's Precise (GPS) and Coarse (network-based) location.

  • Foreground Only: Location data is accessed only while the Application is actively in use on your screen. We do not track or collect your location in the background.
  • Usage: Your location is used locally to display your position on the map, calculate navigation routes, query nearby places via Google Maps API, and fetch weather information.

B. Camera Access (100% On-Device Processing)

The Application requests access to your device's rear camera for two specific optional features:

  • Parking Spot Scanner ("Park Yeri Tara"): Runs real-time object detection (using TensorFlow.js) on your screen to identify vehicle availability on the kerb.
  • Stop Guidance ("Durak Yönlendirmesi"): Displays an augmented backdrop behind navigation indicators to help you locate public transit stops visually.
Important Privacy Guarantee: All camera video processing is performed 100% locally on your device in real time. No video streams, camera frames, photos, or recordings are saved, stored, or transmitted to any server or third party.

C. Anonymous Usage Analytics & Diagnostic Data

We collect minimal, non-personally identifiable diagnostic and usage telemetry to improve application stability and optimize user experience.

  • Anonymous Identifier: A randomly generated anonymous installation ID (provided via Firebase Anonymous Authentication) is associated with analytics events. This identifier does not contain or link to personal identity, email, or device serial numbers.
  • Event Data Collected: Screen views (e.g., home screen, search screen), feature interactions (e.g., route calculated, favorite added), app launch timestamps, and session durations.
  • Device Diagnostics: Device model, manufacturer, Android OS version, and application version.
  • Crash Reports: Uncaught error stack traces for diagnosing application crashes.
  • Opt-Out Control: You can enable or disable usage analytics at any time directly within the Application via Profile > Usage Analytics.

D. Local Storage Data

For your convenience, your preferences (e.g., language selection, theme choices, saved favorite places, recent search history, and optional display name) are stored locally on your device. This data remains on your phone and is not transmitted to our servers.

2. Third-Party Services

The Application integrates with trusted third-party service providers to deliver navigation and analytical capabilities. These third parties process data according to their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Maps Platform (Google LLC): Used for map rendering, place search (Places API), and routing calculations. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): Used for anonymous authentication and Firestore storage of aggregated usage metrics. Firebase Privacy & Security Information.
  • Open-Meteo API: Used to fetch current weather conditions based on approximate location coordinates. Open-Meteo Terms & Privacy.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal data. We do not display third-party advertisements within the Application, nor do we integrate advertising SDKs (such as AdMob or tracking beacons).

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not require user accounts, we do not store personal profiles on remote servers. All local user data (saved places, search history) can be permanently deleted by clearing the Application's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the Application.

5. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take immediate corrective measures.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be effective immediately upon publishing the updated Privacy Policy on this page.
